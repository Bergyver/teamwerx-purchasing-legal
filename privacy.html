TeamWerx Purchasing App: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

The TeamWerx Purchasing App is an internal tool used only by Screen Medic.

What We Access From QuickBooks

Vendor details, bills, bill payments (dates, amounts, method, check or reference numbers) and attachments on those records. The data comes from Screen Medic's own QuickBooks Online company, and only after an owner connects it.

How We Use It

Only to run the app's own features, like tracking vendor orders, matching payments to bills, and showing what was paid and when. We don't sell, share or rent this data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who Can See It

Only Screen Medic owners. Office users don't see cost or QuickBooks data.

How It's Protected

The data is stored on Screen Medic's own server. Access tokens are encrypted, sign-in requires a company Google account, and all traffic is encrypted.

Disconnecting

An owner can disconnect QuickBooks at any time. That revokes the app's access and deletes the stored tokens.
